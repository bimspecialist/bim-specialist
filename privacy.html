Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 31, 2026

BIM Specialist is a productivity toolkit for Autodesk Revit. This policy describes the general privacy approach for the product website and software while the product is in development.

Information

BIM Specialist is designed to use only information necessary to provide its features and licensing functions. Autodesk account identifiers may be used for license entitlement verification when Autodesk Marketplace licensing is enabled.

Project data

BIM project data is processed only as required by the tools selected by the user. The current product is not intended to sell user or project data.

Licensing

License verification may communicate with BIM Specialist licensing services and Autodesk services to determine entitlement status.

Changes

This policy may be updated as production services and Marketplace distribution are finalized.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, use the contact information provided on the BIM Specialist support page.
